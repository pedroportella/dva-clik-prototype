# One-page pitch

I am a senior web developer with strong experience delivering accessible, structured government digital services across Drupal-backed platforms and modern front-end applications. I can contribute immediately to DVA's CLIK program because the role combines the areas I work best in: Drupal 10/GovCMS-aware delivery, structured content migration, metadata and taxonomy design, stakeholder-led publishing workflow, platform health assessment and practical technical planning.

For this application I have prepared a working prototype that reframes a Drupal/Vue government service base into a DVA CLIK operations console. It demonstrates how I would organise the urgent 2,000-page upload stream: batch tracking by CLIK library, workflow state, policy owner, risk and next action; content-quality checks for metadata, broken links, heading structure and duplicates; Drupal health checks covering core/GovCMS posture, custom theme surface, accessibility and performance; and options for aligning CLIK with the broader DVA website and AI-assisted search experience. This reflects how I would work on day one: make the highest-priority upload stream visible, measurable and governable, while leaving a clear audit trail for project leads, policy owners and ICT stakeholders.

My Drupal experience includes site building, configuration, administration and custom development in Drupal 9/10-style architectures, including content entities, fields, Views-style administrative reporting, REST/API integration, custom module patterns, Composer/Drush workflows, Twig theming concepts and decoupled front-end integration. Recent prototype work includes a Drupal 10 backend scaffold with custom module, REST endpoints, document upload handling, admin reporting, Docker local runtime and Vue 3/TypeScript front end. I am comfortable moving between Drupal configuration, PHP/Symfony concepts, front-end implementation, automated testing and delivery documentation.

I understand that CLIK is not just a website; it is an authoritative policy and procedural knowledge library used by staff and clients, and it now underpins CLIKChat. That makes content accuracy, currency, metadata, accessibility and publishing discipline operationally important. I would approach the migration by defining repeatable templates and acceptance criteria, validating content before publication, prioritising based on business need, confirming ownership decisions, and reporting blockers early. For the audit stream, I would combine automated checks with editorial review patterns so technical debt, stale content, accessibility issues and IA gaps become a prioritised remediation roadmap rather than an unstructured findings list.

I have strong communication and planning skills and work comfortably across technical and non-technical groups. I can run focused content clinics for editors, translate technical risks into options papers for decision-makers, manage tickets with clear triage and escalation, and work autonomously while keeping delivery leads informed. My style is pragmatic: stabilise the time-critical work first, document decisions, improve the platform safely, and give DVA a maintainable path for CLIK's longer-term alignment with the main DVA website and responsible AI-supported service delivery.
